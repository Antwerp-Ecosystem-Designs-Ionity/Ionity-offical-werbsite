## 8. Keyword Integration Guidelines

### DO
✅ Use trending keywords naturally in headlines and first paragraphs  
✅ Create dedicated resource pages for complex trending concepts  
✅ Use variations (e.g., "Edge AI," "AI at the Edge," "On-device Intelligence")  
✅ Link trending concepts to your specific solutions  
✅ Monitor search volume trends for emerging opportunities  

### DON'T
❌ Keyword stuff or artificially inflate keyword density  
❌ Use outdated terms just because they're in old marketing material  
❌ Ignore semantic variations and user intent  
❌ Create pages solely for keyword targeting without genuine value  
❌ Neglect mobile optimization for trending searches
