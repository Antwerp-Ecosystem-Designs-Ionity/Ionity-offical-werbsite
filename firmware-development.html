SDK & AGENTIC CORE

1. Scope Redefinition

Monitoring vs. Control

Our strategy shifts from control (SCADA) to intelligence (CMS & PdM). We are building a passive, high-fidelity F.it (Forensic Intelligence Information Technology) observation layer where the SDK acts as a "Digital Reliability Engineer".

The goal is not to move the valve, but to predict the valve's failure 200 hours before it happens using real-time DAQ streams.

FOCUS: INTELLIGENCE ROLE: PREDICTION

2. DAQ & CMS Inputs

The Sensory Input

The SDK’s Agentic Core treats every sensor as a "data document", handling the planning of how to slice data for analysis.

  • High-Speed DAQ: Radxa Penta SATA HAT + SSD stack for vibration/thermal telemetry.
  • Pattern Recognition: Autonomous CMS executing Python-based FFT on the fly to monitor "Bearing harmonics".
INPUT: SENSORY TECH: FFT / PYTHON

3. PdM Workflows

Multi-turn Reasoning

The Copilot SDK excels at multi-turn reasoning, the "missing link" in standard PdM.

It acts as the bridge that transforms raw diagnostic data into forensic intelligence and actionable maintenance foresight.
