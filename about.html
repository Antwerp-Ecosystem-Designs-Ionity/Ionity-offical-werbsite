OUR STORY // ORIGIN
Chapter 01

Our Story

It all started on a late Friday afternoon. Fueled by the excitement of the upcoming weekend, a six-year-old Johan Wilhelm van Antwerp sat mesmerized by the pixelated screensaver of a Windows '95 OS drifting through the digiverse. With the lights off, he stared into that digital depth for hours. Just him and that heavy machine in a dark room — the beginning of a lifelong obsession driven by pure passion.

From burnt CPU cores to formatted hard drives, nothing kept them apart; the digital world was his playspace. As he grew, he kept building and upgrading parts, always choosing to upgrade rather than downgrade or throw away. He grew from a toddler into an adolescent alongside his “Digital Twin” in ways others couldn’t quite understand.

This lifelong connection naturally led Johan to become a Product Design Engineer, deeply focused on client feedback and mechanical innovation.
