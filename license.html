Frequently Asked Questions
What does Policy AED 986 protect?

Policy AED 986 establishes absolute asset sovereignty over all Ionity (Pty) Ltd intellectual property, hardware designs, software code, integration schematics, and trade secrets. It governs all work relations with a zero-share default and requires explicit consent for any disclosure.

How does the licensing apply to hardware devices?

All hardware (SBC, MCU, and related components) remains the property of Ionity (Pty) Ltd. Software deployed on these devices is lease-based via company or industry subscriptions. Repairs and maintenance are restricted to Ionity or explicitly authorized partners.

What is the Creative Commons 4.0 license scope?

The BY-NC-SA 4.0 license applies strictly to content explicitly designated as shareable, such as select open-source scripts. This license is always overruled by Policy AED 986 for all industrial core work, proprietary systems, and trade secrets.

What happens if unauthorized access or tampering is detected?

Ionity maintains the Right of Removal — the authority to auto-deactivate, purge, or reclaim hardware and data assets immediately. In "Alarm Principle" scenarios, emergency script purge is triggered. A $1/KB penalty applies to unauthorized data leaks.

How do I get enterprise-level protection?
