Sensing // RF Intelligence

WiFi CSI & BLE Sensing

Ionity's proprietary WiFi Channel State Information (CSI) and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) scanning platform transforms standard wireless infrastructure into a high-precision sensing layer — no cameras, no wearables, zero privacy intrusion.

WiFi CSI Presence Detection

Our firmware extracts sub-carrier amplitude and phase data from standard WiFi signals to detect human presence, movement patterns, and room occupancy without any on-body devices.

Health & Biometric Monitoring

Advanced FFT-based signal processing extracts breathing rate and heartbeat signatures from CSI variance data — enabling contactless health monitoring for elderly care, hospitals, and secure facilities.

BLE Device Scanning

Continuous BLE scanning for asset tracking, proximity triggers, and device inventory. Detect, classify, and locate BLE-enabled equipment across your facility in real-time.

RF Security & Threat Detection

Monitor the wireless spectrum for rogue access points, unauthorized BLE devices, and signal anomalies. Our AI correlates RF fingerprints against known baselines for automated intrusion alerts.

Spatial Analytics & Indoor Navigation

Combine CSI + BLE + RSSI for sub-meter indoor positioning and spatial analytics. Generate heatmaps, traffic flow visualizations, and occupancy patterns from raw RF data.

Edge-Native Processing

All CSI/BLE data is processed on-device via ESP32-S3 firmware — ensuring sub-millisecond response times, zero cloud dependency, and full offline capability.
