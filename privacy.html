  • To send you relevant updates about our AIoT, Edge AI, and industrial automation services (only with your consent)
  • To comply with legal obligations under South African law, including the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)
  • To protect our rights and enforce our Terms of Service and Policy AED 986

03 Legal Basis for Processing (POPIA & GDPR)

We process your personal information based on the following legal grounds:

  • Consent: Where you have given us explicit consent (e.g., subscribing to communications)

  • Contractual necessity: To fulfil requests you make through our contact forms or service enquiries
  • Legal obligation: To comply with applicable South African laws, including POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013)

06 Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: Request confirmation of whether we hold personal information about you and request access to it
  • Right to correction: Request that we correct or update inaccurate personal information
  • Right to deletion: Request that we delete your personal information where it is no longer necessary
  • Right to object: Object to the processing of your personal information for direct marketing
  • Right to data portability: Request your personal information in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw any previously given consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at johan@ionity.today.

07 Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. Analytics data is retained in anonymised form. Contact form submissions are retained for up to 24 months unless you request earlier deletion.

08 Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • HTTPS encryption across all pages
  • Secure hosting via GitHub Pages with SSL/TLS
  • Google Consent Mode v2 for privacy-respecting analytics
  • Zero-trust security principles as outlined in our infrastructure design

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

09 Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately at johan@ionity.today and we will promptly delete it.
